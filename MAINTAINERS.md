# Maintainers

| Handle | Role |
| --- | --- |
| [@webdevsamran](https://github.com/webdevsamran) | Original Creator / Founder / Lead Maintainer |

## Responsibilities

- Reviewing and merging pull requests (with priority on results,
  methodology, and schema changes).
- Applying trust states (`VERIFIED` / `COMMUNITY_VALIDATED`) to
  submitted results after validation.
- Triaging issues and labeling good first issues.
- Guarding the project's honesty policy: no fabricated hardware,
  metrics, or runtime support.
- Coordinating with runtime maintainers, hardware vendors, and
  researchers.

## Becoming a maintainer

See [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) for the contributor ladder. There is
no automatic promotion; maintainers are added by consensus of existing
maintainers when contributors demonstrate sustained, high-quality
involvement.
