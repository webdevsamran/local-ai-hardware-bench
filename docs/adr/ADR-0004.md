# ADR-0004 — Strict `.aihwbench` bundle verification

**Status:** Accepted (implemented in `339acb5`)

**Context.** `verify_bundle` reported extra unmanifested ZIP members but
returned `valid=true`; manifest grammar was unchecked; no archive safety
caps existed (zip-bomb surface).

**Decision.** Verification defaults to rejecting any member not listed in
`MANIFEST.sha256` (`allow_extra_members=True` is an explicit opt-out).
Manifest lines are strictly validated and duplicates are rejected. Member
count, total uncompressed size and compression ratio are capped.

**Consequences.** Injected/unchecksummed members invalidate a bundle.
Legitimate archives produced by `create_bundle` are unaffected. Tools that
must tolerate wrappers opt in explicitly.
