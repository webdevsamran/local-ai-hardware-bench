# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Everyone participating in this project — issues, PRs, discussions, and
vendor interactions — is expected to behave professionally and in good
faith.

**Examples of acceptable behavior:**

- Technical criticism of code, data, or methodology
- Honest reporting of failures and negative results
- Patience with contributors of varying experience
- Respectful disagreement with cited evidence

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Publishing fabricated benchmarks or misrepresenting hardware
- Sharing others' private information without consent
- Trolling or bad-faith arguments

## Vendor interactions

Vendors are welcome here. In return we ask that no vendor (or their
representatives) pressure maintainers to alter published results. Findings
stand as measured; responses may be published alongside them.

## Enforcement

Report violations by opening a private issue tagged `conduct` or contacting
the maintainer directly. Repeated violations result in blocking from the
repository and its spaces.

This project follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant; where this
document is silent, the Contributor Covenant v2.1 applies.
